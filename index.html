Create "magic" on the screen with your hands!

How to Play

Use your right hand for effects and your left hand for controls.

Sparkles · Right hand
Keep your fingers close together and wave your hand to create sparkles.
Stars · Right hand
Point your index finger upward and hold it still briefly to place a star.
Magic circles · Right hand
Start with your fingers together, then spread them as you lift your hand.
Color schemes · Left hand
Raise your index finger for scheme 1. Raise your index and middle fingers for scheme 2.
Clear · Left hand
Hold a fist for one second to clear stars and magic circles. Release before clearing again.

Keep your hand visible with your palm facing the camera. Good lighting helps. Press Esc or click outside this window to close it.
